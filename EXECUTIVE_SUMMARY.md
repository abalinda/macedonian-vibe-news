## 🎯 The Plan in 60 Seconds

### Problem
Vibes.mk is hitting technical limits:
- Gemini API rate limits causing scraper failures
- Limited content diversity (missing politics, original content)
- Simple homepage not engaging enough
- No testing/CI pipeline = risky deployments

### Solution
7-phase upgrade over 12 weeks:
1. **Backend Migration** - Switch to Groq API (cheaper, faster)
2. **Vibes News** - New category for original journalism
3. **Homepage Redesign** - Multi-section layout (like ground.news)
4. **Politics Coverage** - Balanced, neutral political news
5. **CI/CD Setup** - Automated testing and deployment
6. **UX Improvements** - Better reading/personalization
7. **Analytics** - Data-driven content decisions

### Outcome
Modern, scalable news platform that:
- Costs 40% less to run
- Publishes more diverse content
- Engages users better
- Deploys safely and quickly
- Maintains "good vibes" philosophy

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Groq quality < Gemini | High | A/B test, keep fallback |
| Homepage redesign breaks SEO | Medium | Maintain URLs, add metadata |
| CI/CD slows development | Low | Start simple, iterate |
| Politics alienates users | Medium | Strong neutrality filters |
| Time overruns | Medium | Gradual rollout (Option 2) |

**Overall Risk:** Medium-Low with proper planning
